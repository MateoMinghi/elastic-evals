# Code of conduct

We want elastic-evals to be a place where people can ask questions, report problems and propose changes without being treated badly.

## Our standards

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Give and accept feedback graciously. Critique code and designs, and say what you would change.
- Be patient with newcomers and with people working in a second language.
- Keep discussion on topic and free of harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualised language or imagery, and publishing other people's private information.
- Do not post real users' personal data in issues. Redact conversations before you share them.

## Scope

This applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and in any space where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits and contributions that break this code, and may restrict participation of anyone who does so repeatedly or seriously. To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through GitHub (for example by messaging a maintainer or using the repository's private reporting form). Reports are treated confidentially.
